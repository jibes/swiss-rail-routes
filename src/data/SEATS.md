# Seat sides (`seat`, `seatFrom` in templates.json)

The side to sit on for the views, travelling in the chain's order; the app flips it for a ride the
other way (`src/core/seat.ts`). Only where a source says it plainly for that direction. No
operator page states a side: these come from travel guides and forums (checked Nov 2026). Lines
without a clear or agreed answer have no tip: Vereina, Voralpen-Express, Brünig, Gotthard,
Oberalp, Rigi from Arth-Goldau, the Lake Lucerne boat.
