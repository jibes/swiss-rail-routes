# swiss-rail-routes: agent notes

Follows the portfolio standard: https://github.com/jibes/apps-data/blob/main/STANDARD.md
List deviations from it here, each with a reason. Product spec: [`docs/SPEC.md`](docs/SPEC.md). Design: [`docs/DESIGN.md`](docs/DESIGN.md) (principles, tokens, screenshot review).

Status: 0.25.0. One object, one screen, one line (SPEC §3a): every trip dated (new ones today, from now), planned while edited, the same line under way and shared; answers kept on the device; finished trips wait in search. Android shell with trip notifications. MCP server in `mcp/`. Next: SPEC §3 Should/Could items.

## Stack

- TypeScript strict, React 19, Vite, vite-plugin-pwa, zustand, MapLibre GL, idb-keyval, fast-xml-parser, Vitest, Playwright. Node: see `.nvmrc`.
- One Cloudflare Worker in `workers/ojp/` holds the opentransportdata.swiss keys.
- Check library APIs against the installed types in `node_modules`, not from memory.
- Cloud sessions: Playwright uses `CHROMIUM_PATH` (set by the SessionStart hook); never run `playwright install` there.

## Architecture

- `src/core/` is pure logic with no DOM, and every module is covered by tests in `tests/` (most by a file of their own; `rows`, `retire`, `refresh`, `connection`, `ics`, `share`, `b64` inside their neighbours': `line.test.ts`, `tripLink.test.ts`, `export.test.ts`).
- The planner (`src/core/planner/`) gets all I/O injected (`fetchTrips`, `now`, templates, GA rules); tests replay `tests/fixtures/ojp/`.
- `src/core/ojp/request.ts` is shared with the Worker; the Worker builds requests only from typed JSON and never forwards client XML.
- `workers/ojp/src/handler.ts` uses only the standard Fetch API with injected deps (tested in Node); `index.ts` wires Cloudflare bindings.
- OJP facts found in recorded answers (Nov 2026), relied on by `src/core/ojp/parse.ts` and the planner:
  - Only the **first** `<Via>` of a TripRequest is enforced. The planner asks once with all vias and, when no answer passes them all, splits the leg at the vias and stitches the answers (`src/core/planner/stitch.ts`).
  - A leg cannot end where it starts; round trips without a stop turn at the middle one of your own stations, never at a line's ring (`splitLoops`).
  - Category `PE` is also used for regular trains (Luzern-Interlaken Express). Reservation-only panorama trains carry attribute `A__PR`; product names (`BERNINA EXPRESS`) are matched against `ga.json`.
  - Cog railways come as PtMode `telecabin`/`funicular` with category `CC`; replacement buses as `EV`.
  - OJP passes the first via by its exact stop, walking through the station when the train or bus stops beside it (EV at "Bergün/Bravuogn, posta" → walk to Bergün/Bravuogn → back; boat to "Luzern Bahnhofquai" → walk to Luzern). So a via counts as passed when any section, walks included, reaches it (`visited` in `connection.ts`); the line folds walks under a minute, and a walk out and back to the same stop, into one row with both stops (`rows.ts`: Bergün is one row, arriving and leaving, not two 8-minute walks). Walks carry their minutes (`walkMin`, from `<Duration>`), and a connection's dep/arr include walks at its ends.
  - Cancelled trains and stops not served (`Cancelled`, `NotServicedStop`) are dropped from trip answers.
  - OJP marks no train running on (no stay-seated element in any recorded answer): the same journey keeps its `JourneyRef`; one renumbered at a stop is two rides. `runsOn` (`src/core/planner/connection.ts`): the same journey, or two rail rides at the same stop within a minute from the same track (sector letters left out: `2AB`, `2CD`) are one train: no change counted (`changesOf`), no tight transfer, no platform line, no depart/exit notice; the station says "The train continues as S1" (`continuesAs` in `src/core/changePlatform.ts`). A journey OJP does not know (`TRIPINFO_NOJOURNEYFOUND`) is `UnknownJourneyError`, the MCP's `train_status` answers `unknown_journey`, not an outage.
  - Asked on the day, an answer carries `<EstimatedTime>` beside `<TimetabledTime>` (one recorded, Bern → Interlaken Ost on 4 Nov 2026, `tests/fixtures/ojp/estimates/live.xml.gz`, with a train that does not serve Bern; `tests/estimates.test.ts`, which also makes its own). `tests/liveFacts.test.ts` (report only, skipped without `OJP_LIVE_URL`; run by `live.yml`) says whether same-day answers still carry them and `<Cancelled>` / `<NotServicedStop>` (seen 4 Nov 2026), and `LIVE_FACTS_SAVE=<file>` keeps one. `StopCall.dep/arr` and `Connection.dep/arr` are always the timetable's (links, calendar, the question asked again by `refresh.ts`); a delay known is `depEst/arrEst` beside them (0.15.0). The planner chains legs on the expected times (`expectedDep/expectedArr` in `connection.ts`) and says a change tight across two legs as `warningsFor` says one within a connection; the live view shows the plan's delay until the train reports, then what it reports, on time included; notifications come for the expected times. Platforms as times (0.17.2): `platform` is the timetable's (`PlannedQuay`), what links, calendar and print carry; one the service already knew another (`EstimatedQuay`) is `platformEst` beside it, the only one where no plan came. The ride shows the train's report, else `platformEst`, else the plan's, and a change against the plan as both (`platformAt`, `platformMoved` in `src/core/live.ts`); one taken back shows nothing. Each ride's `destination` is the service's `DestinationText`.
  - A line closed that day (Mürren–Grütschalp–Lauterbrunnen, 2–3 Nov 2026) answers nothing at all, with or without a via. The planner's ladder for a leg: all vias and lines' modes; else the vias that have a connection from the one before (`reachableRules`), one request each; only then none.
  - A situation names the train (`AffectedVehicleJourney`), not the stops. Whether a notice concerns the stretch you ride is read from its words, "zwischen A und B" in four languages (`onThisStretch` in `src/core/warnings.ts`); one about another stretch of the train's route is `disruption-away`: said in blue, not counted against the plan. A stretch counts only when you ride it (0.18.1, reported on the MCP: VAE St. Gallen–Luzern flagged for Luzern–Ebikon, IC1 Lausanne–Zürich for Lausanne–Genève-Aéroport): both ends among the ride's calls, or one of them and the other within 2 km of its track and not beyond either end (an express passing it; `place` is `placeByName` in `src/core/stops.ts`, injected into the planner and refresh). A replacement bus (`EV`) whose ends lie by two stations of a line the leg rides says that line's stretch is by road (`replacement-bus`, `lineReplaced`): the Albula closure in T1 is said that way, not as the trains' notice. Such a bus passes the line's stations between its ends by road (`Section.byRoad`, set by the planner's `markByRoad`; `visited` and `plannedRows` count them): the Albula's express bus Thusis–Preda (6 Nov 2026, `B1R`) rides the line, where it counted as missing Filisur and Bergün and the line asked for was planned via the Vereina (reported on the MCP, 5 Nov 2026). Stops are looked up by `stopKey`, as a bus stand's ref carries its quay. A leg whose line still cannot be ridden says so as an error naming it and its stations (`line-missed`, `lineMissed`, scored well behind one that rides it); the MCP never recommends such an option while another rides the line, and with `strict` answers `line_not_ridden`, no options and why. A ride touching one end of a stretch only at its first or last call (0.21.5) is on it when it lies inside (its far end nearer each named place than they are to each other: the Mont-Blanc Express Les Marécottes–Vallorcine for "Martigny–Vallorcine"), else away (from the other side: Olten–Luzern for "Luzern–Ebikon"), and away when where things are is not known. A notice names a stretch also as "from X" ("ab Erstfeld", dès, da, from) or "up to X" (bis, jusqu'à, fino a): from X onwards in the direction of travel (a ride ending at or before X is elsewhere), and the warning names that stretch (`wDisruptionFrom`, `noticeStretch`, `stretchHere`). A situation weighs by its words (`gradeNotice` in `src/core/noticeGrade.ts`, `NoticeInfo.grade`, 0.21.2, retest of 5 Nov 2026: a 1-min shift "ab Erstfeld" was an error): OJP's own `Severity`/`Priority`/`Planned` say normal/3/true on every recorded situation (63), so headline and consequences decide, in four languages: closed or cancelled an error, restricted, replaced, rerouted, late or a stop left out a warning, anything else ("Information zu …", reservation advice) info; a closure ridden by its own replacement bus only a warning; scored by grade (error 1, warn 0.5, info 0.1 of `disruption`), and the facts line counts warn and error only. A ride's track without its geometry (the MCP drops tracks) is drawn through its stops by `stopKey`, as a ref carries its quay: the track was empty and every notice "on" (Thusis–Preda said on the IR38 from Preda). A situation's validity, the stops it names and the operator's description and remarks are kept beside its headline (`Section.noticeInfo`: `until`, `affected`, `detail`), shown when the notice is opened and in the MCP (`validUntil`, `affected`, `detail`). A day trip back on a later day than it left, past 03:00, is dropped while another is back the same day, else said (`next-day`, `sameDayFirst`). A scenic line's own stretch ridden after sunset + `DUSK_MIN` (15 min) at its middle station is said from when it is dark (`line-after-dark`, `linesAfterDark` in `src/core/warnings.ts`, weight 20: of otherwise equal options the one by day; 0.20.0, reported on the MCP: a 16:05 Gotthard option on the mountain line by night). A stay's window (`Waypoint.leaveBy`, `arriveAfter`, `HH:MM` on the trip's date): a leg leaves a stop by its `leaveBy`; once any option leaves in time, those that cannot are not offered (`inTime`, 0.21.5); only when none can, the connections there are each said as late (`window-missed`, error, weight 120, `leavingBy` in `src/core/planner/plan.ts`), and a stay starts at its `arriveAfter` at the earliest; links carry them (`#/r/` modifiers `f<hhmm>`/`u<hhmm>`, `#/s/` slots 9 and 10; the old never-enforced slots 7 and 8 and modifiers `a`/`b` stay read past), riding through drops them (`setEntryStay`), the panel sets leave by (`src/ui/LeaveBy.tsx`, `setEntryLeaveBy`) and the line says it ("Stay 30 min, by 17:30"). Waiting after the time asked for costs as travel does (0.21.6, round 3, 6 Nov 2026: Nyon–La Cure from 09:00 recommended the 13:29 for a ride a minute shorter than the 09:29; Zürich–Gotthard–Lugano from 08:00 the 10:05): a chain scores its time from the asked time, arrive-by its time to the asked arrival (`planFrom`, `plan`). A notice its words grade as information is a `notice` ("Notice A–B"), a reservation advice `reservation-advised`, neither a disruption, both blue (`NOTICE_TYPES` open the operator's text). A replacement bus is category `EV` or a bus whose line is named so (`EV2`, category `B`: the Mont-Blanc Express, `isReplacementBus`); its ends are placed along the line's course through its stations (`lineStretch`), not only at them. `stopKey` reduces a UIC number with OJP's generated platform part (`8301003_gen:missingSLOID_pf:31`, Domodossola said not reached). GA and day-pass notes (`not-ga`, `ga-unknown`) are for those tickets only; the app's ticket is the GA. A tight change at a line's `timedChanges` station (Grütschalp: the train waits for the cable car) is `timed-change`, info, not scored; the live view's risk is unchanged. A leg whose next connection in the same answers leaves over `GAP_MIN` (120 min) after the planned one says when (`sparse`, info, not scored, `gapWarning` in `src/core/planner/plan.ts`); nothing when the answers hold none later. Stops abroad DIDOK does not list (Chamonix-Mont-Blanc, Vallorcine, Domodossola) are in the stop index by their UIC number (`UIC_MIN`, `EXTRA` in `scripts/build-stops.ts`) and keep it as ref and key, as OJP refuses `ch:1:sloid:<uic>` (`stopKey`); the GA and day passes end at Le Châtelard-Frontière (`ABROAD` in `scripts/build-ga.ts`), and a GA note names the stretch uncovered, not the whole ride (`gaStretchOf`).

Fares: none (SPEC §4: the OSDM offline delivery is the international tariff, in EUR, from 2023).
- Curated data: `src/data/templates.json` (types in `src/core/data.ts`; a line is enforced by its must-pass stations and its modes, nothing else: the avoided-segment rule was redundant and went in 0.6.3). `ga.json` is generated (`npm run data:ga`) from SBB's OSDM fares; it covers fares SBB sells, so other operators' summit lines (Niesen, Harder…) are missing, never guessed. Unknown is never covered (0.21.5, retest round 3: Interlaken Ost → Jungfraujoch said nothing): a cable car or cog railway with neither a rule nor evidence of cover says `ga-unknown` (info, not scored; `gaUnknown` in `src/core/warnings.ts`); the evidence is `ga.covered`, the summit stretches with a GA fare of 0 in the same OSDM data (Rigi, Grütschalp…), each end with the stops within 1 km. A fare names a place's main station; where its two ends share no mode, the generator moves an end to the nearest stop within 1 km that has the other's (Mürren BLM → Mürren (Schilthornbahn), Zermatt → Zermatt GGB), as rides are matched by the stops they call at (`tests/ga.test.ts`). Highlight names are in the four languages (`name` per language, a plain string still read, `highlightName`); warnings and notices carry the highlight's id (`highlight`) and its English name, and the screen, notifications and MCP put it in theirs (`localParams`). Which way a line runs where it is put (`lineReversed` in `src/core/lineDirection.ts`, the add sheet and the MCP): towards the station after it when that is one of its ends (Zürich, Voralpen-Express, Luzern runs from St. Gallen), else from the station before when that is one, else from the end nearer it. Seat sides (`seat`, from `seatFrom` when only part of a line has the view) are in chain order, with their sources in `src/data/SEATS.md`; `src/core/seat.ts` flips them for a ride the other way (the ride's row, and a highlight's notification). Must-pass stops must be stops trains call at (OJP lists only those): Wassen, for one, is not. Each line added since 0.19.0 has an acceptance test planning it end to end on recorded answers that calls at every must-pass stop (`L*` in `tests/acceptance.test.ts`: LMBE, LNSTCM, LCENTO, LMOB, LVIGNES); a new line gets one, recorded with `record:ojp:missing`.
