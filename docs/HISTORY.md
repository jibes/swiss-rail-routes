# History

What each piece of the app was, and why, release by release: the notes AGENTS.md kept until 0.61.0, moved here as they were. Where they disagree with AGENTS.md ("The app as it is"), AGENTS.md is right: many describe features since removed (0.58.0–0.61.0: fares in the app, Keep map for offline, the whole run, the map planner, Find one that works, typed lists, Back the same way, Reverse direction, the reservation setting, pins, Update/Discard under way, the live bar, the third column, the map's weather).

Stores: `src/trips.ts` (`srr.trips.v1`; route edits save at once and drop the timetable) and `src/tours.ts` (legacy, `srr.tours.v1`: past trips kept as routes before 0.52.0, read once by `migratePastTrips` in `src/io/migratePast.ts` and synced until then), both on `src/io/listStore.ts`: saved item by item into what is stored (one IndexedDB transaction, so a stale tab never drops another tab's items), other tabs told on every save; what is unsaved when the page goes is left in localStorage (`srr.unsaved.trips.v1`, `srr.unsaved.tours.v1`: IndexedDB cannot finish a write then) and taken into the list on the next load (a write that finishes after all takes its change out again, `settleUnsaved`); a list that could not be read or written is never written or synced again that visit (`failed`; a failed write rejects, so a sync round never counts it as saved).
- Links: `#/s/<data>` a trip as its trains (`src/core/export/tripLink.ts`, a few hundred characters: messengers cut off longer ones; since 0.21.0 the compact form, `tripLinkPacked.ts` with the format in `tripPack.ts`: stations by SLOID/UIC number, the stable key, never a row of the bundled index; ride ends by station (`stopKey`), platforms and products left to refresh; journeys packed by form (`…:<n>-<run>`, `…:plan:<n>`, `…:plan:<uuid>`), since 0.22.6 (header `0x30`; `0x20` still read) only where the next ride is the same train (written once, the next saying "the same"), so a train running on stays one; the others come back when it opens, `refresh.ts` finding a ride without its journey by its stations, departure minute and line; a ride without a line carries its number as one (T1: 337 for 423, `tests/fixtures/links/v1-T3.txt` a 0.21 link that must still open); a CRC-16, and `z` at the end, as base64url can end in `-`/`_`, which link detectors drop: the cause of the reported broken links. About a third of the JSON form's length (T1, 14 rides: 337 for 939). The JSON form (`encodeTripLinkV1`, kept for tests) still opens, also with that dropped character put back; a compact link cut short or changed opens as damaged, never as another trip), the rest asked again when it opens (`src/core/refresh.ts`, which is also pull-down reload, `src/ui/PullReload.tsx`); `#/j/<data>` a trip with all its timetable still opens; route links (`#/r/<stops>?…`, `src/core/export/routeLink.ts`; older `#/t/<base64>`; a 0.3 route address `#/tour/<id>?s=…`) open as a route to plan as a new trip (`SharedTour`; without a date, or with one gone, today). Per-station time windows (`earliestArr`/`latestDep`, link modifiers `a`/`b`, link slots 7 and 8) were never enforced and went in 0.15.1; old links are read past them.
- Sync (SPEC §3b): `src/core/sync.ts` (pure: key, HKDF, AES-GCM, stamps, merge; a trip's hash leaves out its timetable, so planning is no change and never outdates a route edited elsewhere), `src/io/sync.ts` (engine; `srr.sync.v1`; a round gives up on the tabs' lock after `LOCK_WAIT_MS`, so a tab frozen mid-round never holds the others for good; an item's unknown fields are read, saved and hashed as they came (`toursList`'s `read`), so a field a newer version adds is neither lost nor an edit here; an item this version cannot read at all is held as it came (`held` in `src/io/listStore.ts`): never shown, written back untouched, and part of every sync round, so it is never deleted or turned into a tombstone (0.15.5)), `src/ui/Settings.tsx`, `src/ui/QrScanner.tsx` (BarcodeDetector, else lazy jsQR; e2e feeds a fake camera from an MJPEG, `e2e/scan.spec.ts`); Worker `workers/ojp/src/sync.ts` + Durable Object `SyncBox` (a new key closes the old box with a marker; a box never written stays nothing, and closing counts as a write for the limiter). e2e answers `/sync/` with the Worker's own `syncHandle` and an in-memory store (`answerWorker` in `e2e/fixtures.ts`).
- Kept answers (0.8.4): every timetable answer the planner gets is kept on the device (`src/io/answers.ts`, database `srr.answers.v1`, one entry per query), good until its day starts and, on the day, for 15 min (delays, cancellations; `src/core/answers.ts`); about 8 MB at most, the oldest dropped, tidied after the first kept a visit and every 50 after (0.52.1). The Worker keeps a trip answer 10 min, one for the day itself 1 min (`SAME_DAY_TRIP_CACHE`, 0.52.1). The options loaded with the strip's arrows are kept per trip until they leave (`srr.options.v1`), and a trip's saved plan still shows its options after a connection is chosen or the trip renamed (`optionsKey`: stations, day, ticket).
- Reservation trains (Settings, `srr.prefs.v1`, on this device, not synced): `PlanDeps.reservations` is `avoid` (default; also when absent, so other callers of `plan()` see no change), `include` or `never`. `planRoute` passes it and marks the result; a result planned under another choice is never kept, saved plans of another choice are not reused, and options loaded with the arrows are keyed by it (`optionsKey`). Changing it drops the timetable of planned trips that have not started (`replanUpcoming`); trips under way keep theirs.
